# 🎬 Amazon Prime Video — EDA

## 📌 Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on Amazon Prime Video's content catalog to understand patterns in movies, TV shows, genres, ratings, countries, runtime, and popularity.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze Movies vs TV Shows
- Study content growth over the years
- Identify popular and highly-rated genres
- Analyze IMDb and TMDB ratings
- Study runtime and TV show seasons
- Analyze content-producing countries
- Identify relationships between ratings and popularity
- Generate data-driven business recommendations

## 📂 Datasets

### `titles.csv`
Contains information about movies and TV shows.

**9,871 rows × 15 columns**

### `credits.csv`
Contains information about cast, characters, and directors.

**124,235 rows × 5 columns**

## 📊 Key Insights

- Movies make up the majority of the catalog.
- The catalog has expanded significantly in recent years.
- TV shows generally have higher IMDb ratings than movies.
- More than 65% of TV shows have only one season.
- Drama, Comedy, Thriller, and Action are among the most common genres.
- Documentary, History, and Animation show strong average ratings.
- The United States is the largest content-producing country.
- IMDb votes and TMDB popularity have a positive relationship.
- IMDb and TMDB scores also show a meaningful positive correlation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Invest more in strong multi-season TV content.
- Use ratings and engagement when evaluating content.
- Focus on high-performing genres.
- Expand international content partnerships.
